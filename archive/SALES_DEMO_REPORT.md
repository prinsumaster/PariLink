## The 30-Minute Business Story

The sales demonstration sequence has been validated to ensure every module flows seamlessly and tells a compelling business story, proving immediate ROI to prospective clients.

### 1. The Hook: Fleet Dashboard (0-5 minutes)
- **Action:** Open the Executive Dashboard.
- **Narrative:** "This is your business at a glance."
- **Validation:** Visual metrics (Total Revenue, Active Trips, Idle Trucks) render instantly via optimized aggregation queries, establishing PariLink as a lightning-fast enterprise OS.

### 2. The Core Operation: Trip Planning & Dispatch (5-15 minutes)
- **Action:** Create a Trip, assign a Driver/Vehicle, and hit Dispatch.
- **Narrative:** "No more WhatsApp groups or Excel sheets. Dispatch happens in 3 clicks."
- **Validation:** The seamless integration of Master Data (Vehicles/Drivers) into the Trip form proves that PariLink eliminates duplicate data entry.

### 3. The 'Wow' Factor: Control Tower & GPS (15-20 minutes)
- **Action:** Switch to the live Control Tower map.
- **Narrative:** "Real-time visibility without calling the driver."
- **Validation:** Live telemetry pins render on the map component. The AI Copilot surfaces expected delays visually, demonstrating predictive logistics capability.

### 4. The ROI Generator: Billing & Invoices (20-25 minutes)
- **Action:** Transition a completed trip to an Invoice.
- **Narrative:** "Turn deliveries into cash instantly. No more waiting 14 days to collect physical PODs."
- **Validation:** One-click invoice generation validates financial automation.

### 5. The Close: Settings & Subscription (25-30 minutes)
- **Action:** Show the Settings and License Usage Banner.
- **Narrative:** "PariLink scales with you. No hidden user fees, just pay for the trucks you run."
- **Validation:** The UI clearly communicates the capacity limits, reinforcing the transparent pricing model.
