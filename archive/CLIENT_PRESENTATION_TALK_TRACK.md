# PariLink Enterprise 2.0 — Client Presentation Talk Track

## 1. Login & Executive Dashboard
**WHAT TO SHOW:**  
Login screen followed by the Control Tower / Dashboard view with high-level KPI cards and AI insights.
**WHAT TO SAY:**  
"Welcome to PariLink Enterprise. Our platform offers secure, role-based access control. Upon logging in, management gets immediate access to the Executive Dashboard—a true command center. Instead of managing operations across spreadsheets, messaging apps, and fragmented software, you get a single live operational view."
**BUSINESS VALUE:**  
Faster decision-making, better visibility, and proactive management of exceptions.
