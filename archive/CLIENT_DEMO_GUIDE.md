# PariLink Enterprise 2.0 — Client Demo Guide

## Recommended Demonstration Flow

### 1. Pre-Demo Setup
- Ensure the local stack is running (API on port 8080, Web on port 3000).
- URL to open: **http://localhost:3000**
- Login Credentials: 
  - **Email**: `admin@parilink.com`
  - **Password**: `password123`

### 2. A. Login & Identity
- **Action**: Enter credentials and log in.
- **Narrative**: "PariLink Enterprise provides secure, role-based access control. As an administrator, you get immediate access to the Executive Dashboard upon login."

### 3. B. Executive Dashboard
- **Action**: Highlight the high-level KPI cards and AI insights.
- **Narrative**: "The Control Tower gives an instant operational overview. You can see active trips, delayed shipments, and AI-driven business health vitals updated in real-time."

### 4. C. Customer Management
- **Action**: Navigate to **Customers** via the sidebar.
- **Narrative**: "PariLink acts as your central CRM for logistics. Here we manage our key accounts like Acme Global Logistics and TechTrans Supply."

### 5. D. Fleet & Driver Management
- **Action**: Navigate to **Fleet > Drivers** and then **Fleet > Vehicles**.
- **Narrative**: "Managing your assets is seamless. Drivers like Sarah Connor are tracked by their current status (On Trip, Available). Similarly, vehicles are monitored for maintenance and active duty."

### 6. E. Trips & Operations
- **Action**: Navigate to **Trips** or **Dispatch Workspace**. View the active trip for `TRP-1001` (Dallas to Austin).
- **Narrative**: "This is where the magic happens. We can see a load currently in transit from Dallas to Austin, assigned to Sarah Connor driving the Volvo truck. Status updates flow in real-time."

### 7. F. Invoicing & Finance
- **Action**: Navigate to **Finance > Invoices**.
- **Narrative**: "Once a load is delivered, billing is automated. Here we see a paid invoice for Acme Global Logistics, and a newly issued invoice for a completed trip."

### 8. G. Copilot / AI (If applicable)
- **Action**: Navigate to **AI > Prompt Studio** or invoke Copilot.
- **Narrative**: "Our AI Copilot assists dispatchers with predictive routing and anomaly detection, streamlining daily operations."

### 9. H. Final Summary & Logout
- **Action**: Click the profile menu and log out.
- **Narrative**: "PariLink Enterprise scales from a few vehicles to thousands, providing complete visibility and financial control."

---

## ⚠️ Known Demo Limitations
- Do not attempt to process real credit card payments in this local sandbox.
- Real-time GPS telematics are mocked for the demo; live tracking map will display static placeholder locations.
