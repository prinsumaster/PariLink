# PariLink 2.0 Customer Success SOPs

## 1. Onboarding Timeline (7 Days)
- **Day 1:** Send Welcome Email & Onboarding Kit.
- **Day 2:** Discovery call & Master Data CSV review.
- **Day 3:** Run data import. Validate Company structure.
- **Day 5:** Team Training (Dispatchers, Drivers, Accountants).
- **Day 7:** Go-Live & Systems Cutover.

## 2. Training Video Links
- **Admin Setup:** https://parilink.com/training/admin
- **Dispatcher Operations:** https://parilink.com/training/dispatcher
- **Driver App Usage:** https://parilink.com/training/driver
- **Invoicing & Finance:** https://parilink.com/training/finance

## 3. Support Ticket Escalation
- **Tier 1 (L1):** Basic 'how-to' queries, password resets, plan upgrades. Resolved by CS Rep within 1 hour.
- **Tier 2 (L2):** Integration failures, hardware/GPS mapping issues. Escalated to Technical Support.
- **Tier 3 (L3):** Platform bugs, data corruption, downtime. Escalated directly to Engineering On-Call.
