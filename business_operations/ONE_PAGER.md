# PariLink 2.0 — Product One Pager

**The Operating System for Modern Logistics**

## What is it?
PariLink 2.0 is a B2B SaaS platform that unifies fleet dispatch, GPS telemetry, and financial invoicing into a single, real-time control tower.

## Core Value Proposition
- **Recover Working Capital:** Instantly generate invoices when a driver uploads a POD via the offline-first mobile app.
- **Real-Time Visibility:** Live Control Tower mapping overlays GPS telemetry with business dispatch data.
- **AI Intelligence:** Predictive delay alerts and anomalous fuel drop detection.

## Target Audience
Mid-market Indian transport companies operating fleets of 50 to 1,000 trucks.

## Onboarding
Fully self-serve 10-step wizard. Go-live in 48 hours.
