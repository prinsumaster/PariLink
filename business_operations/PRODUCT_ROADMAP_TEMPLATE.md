# PariLink 2.0 — Product Roadmap Framework

*Engineering is frozen. No speculative development is permitted. All future features must be justified using this framework before being approved for development.*

## Feature Proposal Form

**1. Feature Name:** [E.g., Automated E-Way Bill Generation]

**2. Customer Requesting:** [E.g., ABC Logistics, XYZ Freight]

**3. Business Value:** 
[How does this help the customer? E.g., Saves 5 minutes per trip dispatch by removing manual E-Way bill data entry on the government portal.]

**4. Revenue Impact:**
[How does this impact PariLink MRR? E.g., We can charge a ₹5 per E-Way bill API fee, unlocking an estimated ₹250,000/month across 500 active trucks.]

**5. Engineering Estimate:**
[E.g., 2 weeks (1 Backend Eng, 1 Frontend Eng). Requires integration with NIC E-Way Bill API.]

**6. Priority Score (Revenue / Effort):** High / Medium / Low

---
*If a feature does not have a direct Revenue Impact or does not solve a documented Support Escalation, it is rejected.*
