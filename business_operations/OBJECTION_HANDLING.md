# PariLink 2.0 — Objection Handling Guide

## Objection 1: "We already use SAP/Oracle."
**Response:** "SAP is an excellent general ERP, but it's not built for the realities of Indian highways. PariLink is built specifically for transport. You can run PariLink as your logistics execution system and we can pipe the final invoice data directly into SAP via our API."

## Objection 2: "Our drivers don't know how to use apps."
**Response:** "Our Driver App was designed to be as simple as WhatsApp. It's a Progressive Web App (PWA) so they don't even need to visit the Play Store. It works entirely offline and only requires pressing a single large 'Upload POD' button."

## Objection 3: "It's too expensive."
**Response:** "Our pricing is capacity-based. You only pay for the trucks you actively manage. Furthermore, by invoicing just 5 days faster, the software pays for itself in recovered working capital interest alone."
