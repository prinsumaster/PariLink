# Release Notes - PariLink v2.0.1 (Revenue & Scale)

## Summary
The `v2.0.1` release officially completes PariLink's transition from an engineering product to a commercial SaaS platform. There are **zero new business logic features** in this release. All changes are focused on Customer Acquisition, Operations, and Revenue tracking.

## Commercial Automation
- **SaaS Billing Engine:** Integrated a daily cron script (`scripts/billing-engine.ts`) to automatically manage Trial Expirations, Grace Periods, and Account Suspensions.
- **Automated Communication:** Configured outbound hooks for automated Welcome Emails, Trial Expiry warnings, and Invoice Generation notifications.

## Super Admin Revenue Operations
- **Customer Directory & Health Scores:** The Super Admin dashboard now includes a comprehensive directory of all tenants, evaluating live "Health Scores" (Healthy/Warning/Critical) based on usage telemetry and trip volume.
- **Customer Success Console:** Introduced live readouts for Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR), ARR Run Rate, Churn Risk, and Active Trials.
- **1-Click Provisioning:** Implemented operational flows allowing non-engineers to provision companies, assign licensing tiers (Starter/Growth/Enterprise), and inject demo data instantly.

## Process Governance
- **Customer-Driven Roadmap:** All future engineering development is strictly gated behind the `CUSTOMER_FEEDBACK_BOARD.md`. No speculative development will be merged without an explicit Revenue Impact or Business Value justification tied to a paying customer.
