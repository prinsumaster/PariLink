PariLink · Full Delivery Roadmap · 2026 → 2027

From the Oct 1 pilot to the whole platform

All 118 modules, built and verified properly, is about a 12-month journey — not one month. The core launches Oct 1 2026; the rest ships in quarterly phases through Q3 2027. Each phase is a real, usable release — you sell and pilot as you go, not in one big-bang at the end.

This plan assumes a small team of 2–3 engineers plus AI-assisted build, with every screen verified by screenshot before it counts as done. Solo + one AI agent, the same scope runs materially longer, because broken work has to be caught and redone — which is exactly the pattern we keep hitting.
