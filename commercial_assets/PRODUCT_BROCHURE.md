# PariLink 2.0 — Enterprise Fleet Management OS

## The Problem
Transport companies run on fragmented tools: WhatsApp for dispatch, Excel for accounts, and standalone GPS portals for tracking. This fragmentation causes delayed invoicing, high maintenance costs, and missing proof of deliveries.

## The PariLink Solution
PariLink 2.0 is an all-in-one Operating System built specifically for the Indian logistics sector. It unifies operations, telemetry, and finance into a single pane of glass.

## Key Modules
1. **Control Tower:** Real-time map visibility of your entire fleet, overlaying GPS telemetry with live Trip status.
2. **AI Copilot:** An intelligent assistant that identifies expected delays, anomalous fuel drops, and optimal routing based on historical data.
3. **Automated Invoicing:** Instantly generate GST-compliant invoices the second a Driver uploads a POD via the Mobile App.
4. **Maintenance & Fuel Management:** Track every expense against a specific vehicle and trip to calculate exact profitability.
