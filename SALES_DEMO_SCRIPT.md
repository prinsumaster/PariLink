# PariLink 2.0 — Sales Demo Script

**Audience:** Prospective Enterprise Customers (Transport Company Owners, Fleet Managers)

---

## Introduction (2 mins)
*"Welcome to PariLink 2.0. We're going to show you how you can manage your entire fleet, dispatch operations, and driver expenses from a single, unified platform."*

## 1. The Command Center (5 mins)
*"This is your Command Center. It gives you a bird's-eye view of your entire operation."*
- Show the **Live Map** (if GPS is integrated) or the **Dispatch Board**.
- Highlight the **Universal Search** feature — search for any trip, truck, or driver instantly.
- Show the **License Usage Banner** — explain how pricing scales with their success.

## 2. Trip Creation & Dispatch (5 mins)
*"Let's see how easy it is to dispatch a truck."*
- Navigate to **Trips → Create Trip**.
- Quickly select an Origin, Destination, and Customer.
- Assign a Truck and a Driver.
- Highlight the automated checks (e.g., driver license validity, truck maintenance status).

## 3. Driver Super App (5 mins)
*"Your drivers need powerful tools too. PariLink gives them an app that works offline."*
- Switch to the mobile view.
- Show the driver's itinerary.
- Demonstrate adding a Fuel Expense (FASTag/Cash).
- Show the POD (Proof of Delivery) upload process.

## 4. Finance & Analytics (3 mins)
*"Once the trip is done, invoicing should be automatic."*
- Navigate to **Finance → Invoices**.
- Show the auto-generated invoice for the completed trip.
- Switch to **Analytics** and show the fleet utilization and profit margins.

## Conclusion (2 mins)
*"PariLink scales with you. Whether you have 20 trucks or 2,000, our enterprise platform handles the complexity so you can focus on growth."*
