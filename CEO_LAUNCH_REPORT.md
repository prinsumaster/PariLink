## Executive Overview

PariLink Version 2.0 has successfully completed its transformation from a feature-complete engineering build into a polished, commercial-ready enterprise SaaS product. 

This sprint eliminated technical placeholders, implemented rigorous enterprise licensing, and polished the user experience to meet the premium expectations of India's top logistics providers.

---

## Key Commercial Milestones Achieved

### 1. Enterprise Licensing Engine (The "Commercial Core")
We now have a robust system to enforce truck and driver capacities.
- Super Admins can manage customer licenses from a centralized dashboard.
- Immediate capacity overrides (e.g., granting 10 extra trucks instantly) are supported without changing base plans.
- Temporary capacity boosts (with automatic expiry dates) support seasonal peaks (like Diwali).
- Customers see clear, color-coded capacity warnings (70% and 90%) right in their dashboard to drive upsell conversations.

### 2. Zero-Friction Onboarding
The 10-step `TenantOnboardingWizard` is fully wired, allowing new transport companies to self-serve their setup—from company details to GPS integration—without manual intervention from our team.

### 3. Sales Demo Mode
The architecture is set for one-click demo environments. Sales teams can instantly provision a "Small", "Medium", or "Large" transport company pre-filled with realistic Indian logistics data (trucks, trips, drivers, invoices) to demonstrate the platform's power immediately.

### 4. Premium User Experience
The application feels fast, stable, and premium. Loading skeletons prevent jarring layout shifts, empty states guide the user, and dark mode is flawlessly integrated across all modules.
